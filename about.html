Four decades of honest dairy.

DairyPure Farms began in 1985 with twelve cows and a simple promise: milk you can trust, from people you can meet.

Our story

Today our herd of 180 cows grazes the same Magaliesburg pastures. We milk twice a day, test every batch in our own lab, and bottle within hours so the milk reaches you fresh.

We grow our feed on the farm, recycle our water and keep the herd small enough that every cow is known by name.

Mission and vision

Mission: to supply fresh, safe and nutritious dairy to families, shops and restaurants across Gauteng.

Vision: a farm that feeds its community for generations without wearing out the land.

Orders and delivery

Questions we often hear

Is your milk pasteurised?

Yes. We pasteurise gently to keep it safe while preserving its nutrition and taste.

Do you deliver?

We deliver chilled across Johannesburg, Pretoria and the West Rand. Delivery is free on orders over R500, otherwise R65.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes. Farm tours run on Saturday mornings by appointment. Contact us to book a spot.
